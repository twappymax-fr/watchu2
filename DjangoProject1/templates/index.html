What we do

Over 50 years of integrated impact

For over five decades, Torvu has operated at the intersection of community development, financial inclusion, and human dignity. We don't believe in single-issue solutions — poverty is complex, and so is the work to address it. Our integrated ecosystem brings together healthcare, education, livelihoods, and enterprise under one roof, creating lasting, compounding change.

We work in 14 countries across Asia and Africa, reaching over 145 million people through a unique combination of development programmes, social enterprises, and impact investments. Each intervention is locally-rooted, rigorously tested, and designed to scale. What works in one community becomes a model for the next.

Our approach

Four pathways to lasting empowerment

Torvu's model rests on a conviction that poverty isn't one problem — it's a web of interlocking disadvantages. We've mapped four reinforcing pathways through which communities move from deprivation to dignity.

From strengthening livelihoods and opening access to credit, to delivering essential health and education services, to giving communities a voice in decisions that affect their lives — our pathways work in concert, not isolation.
